# Harbourline Health Cover — Policy Handbook (FICTIONAL)

> **Synthetic test corpus.** Harbourline is a fictional insurer. This handbook was written for the
> Week 2 RAG pilot to exercise clause-level retrieval, supersession and conflict handling. It does
> not describe any real product.

Each clause below is one citable rule. Format: `### <clause_id> <title>`, then a metadata line
`Effective: YYYY-MM-DD | Superseded by: <clause_id or —>`, then the rule text.

## 1. General

### 1.1 Who is covered
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
A Harbourline health plan covers the named policyholder and any dependants listed on the policy schedule. Dependent children are covered until the end of the calendar year in which they turn 25.

### 1.2 Policy number required for plan-specific answers
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Support staff may explain general handbook rules to anyone, but confirmation of plan-specific benefits, limits, balances or claim details requires a valid policy number and identity check for the policyholder.

### 1.3 Privacy of customer records
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Harbourline discloses policy records, claim notes and personal information only to the policyholder or a person they have authorised in writing. Requests for another person's records are declined, even from family members, unless written authority is on file.

## 2. Premiums and payments

### 2.1 Premium due dates
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Premiums are due monthly on the billing date shown on the policy schedule. A 14-day grace period applies; cover continues during the grace period but claims for services in that period are paid only once the overdue premium is received.

### 2.2 Duplicate or incorrect charges
Effective: 2025-06-01 | Superseded by: —
If a premium is collected twice or for the wrong amount, Harbourline refunds the excess to the original payment method within 10 business days of confirming the error.

## 3. Hospital cover

### 3.1 Inpatient admission
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Planned inpatient hospital admissions are covered in a network hospital when pre-approved at least 5 business days before admission. Emergency admissions do not need pre-approval but must be notified within 48 hours.

### 3.2 Hospital excess
Effective: 2026-01-01 | Superseded by: —
An excess of $250 per person per calendar year applies to inpatient hospital admissions. The excess does not apply to dependent children or to emergency admissions.

## 4. Outpatient and specialist care

### 4.1 GP consultations
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Visits to a general practitioner (GP or family doctor) are covered at 100% of the scheduled fee, with no annual limit on the number of visits.

### 4.2 Specialist visits
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: 4.2a
Specialist consultations are covered only for cardiology, orthopaedics and oncology, and only after three GP visits for the same condition within the previous six months. Other specialist types are not covered.

### 4.2a Specialist visits
Effective: 2026-03-01 | Superseded by: —
Specialist consultations, including dermatology, cardiology, orthopaedics, oncology and other medical specialists, are covered at 80% of the scheduled fee when the customer has a written referral from their GP dated within the previous 12 months. Specialist visits without a referral are not covered.

### 4.3 Telehealth consultations
Effective: 2025-09-01 | Superseded by: —
Video and phone consultations with a GP or a referred specialist are covered on the same terms as in-person visits.

### 4.4 Diagnostic imaging and pathology
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
X-rays, ultrasounds, MRI scans and blood tests are covered at 80% when ordered by a GP or specialist. MRI scans require pre-approval.

## 5. Prescriptions

### 5.1 Prescription medicines
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Prescription medicines on the Harbourline formulary are covered after a $15 co-payment per item. Non-formulary medicines are not covered.

## 6. Rehabilitation

### 6.1 Physiotherapy sessions
Effective: 2026-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Physiotherapy is covered for up to 10 sessions per person per calendar year at 70% of the scheduled fee. A referral is not required.

## 7. Extras and travel

### 7.1 Dental care
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Routine dental check-ups and cleaning are covered twice per calendar year. Major dental work such as crowns and implants is not covered.

### 7.2 Optical
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Prescription glasses or contact lenses are covered up to $200 every two years.

### 7.3 Pregnancy and maternity
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Pregnancy and birth-related services are covered after a 12-month waiting period from the policy start date.

### 7.4 Overseas travel medical cover
Effective: 2024-01-01 | Superseded by: 7.4a
Emergency medical treatment while travelling overseas is covered for trips of up to 30 days, to a maximum of $50,000 per trip.

### 7.4a Overseas travel cover withdrawn
Effective: 2026-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Harbourline health plans no longer include any overseas travel cover. Customers travelling abroad are advised to buy separate travel insurance.

## 8. Claims

### 8.1 Claim submission deadline
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the date of service, with an itemised receipt.

### 8.2 Claim processing times
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Complete claims are assessed within 15 business days. If information is missing, Harbourline contacts the customer and the 15-day period restarts when it is received.

## 9. Allied health

### 9.3 Allied health session limits
Effective: 2026-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Allied health services, including physiotherapy, chiropractic and osteopathy, are limited to a combined 6 sessions per person per calendar year at 60% of the scheduled fee.

### 9.4 Mental health counselling
Effective: 2025-06-01 | Superseded by: —
Sessions with a registered psychologist or counsellor are covered for up to 12 sessions per calendar year at 70% of the scheduled fee.

## 10. Exclusions

### 10.1 General exclusions
Effective: 2025-01-01 | Superseded by: —
Cosmetic procedures, experimental treatments, and services provided by a family member are not covered.
